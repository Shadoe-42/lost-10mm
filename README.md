# lost-10mm

**Maintenance guides for people who want to know why.**

Every Miata owner loses at least one 10mm socket per job. This repo is for the person who shrugs, buys another, and keeps going.

## Who this is for

You helped a parent change the oil, or you did a set of brake pads once. You know what a spanner looks like. You want to do more of the work on your own car, and you want guidance you can trust that explains the reasoning, not just the steps.

## What makes these guides different

- **Why before how.** Every guide explains why a task exists and how the mechanism works before it tells you what to turn. Once you understand the system, the steps stop being a recipe you follow and become something you can adapt.
- **Healthy baseline first.** Each guide shows what a good car looks like before it shows what a bad one looks like. Most problems get caught by noticing drift from normal, so you need to know normal.
- **Old next to new.** Every guide that replaces or wears a part shows what new looks like beside what is worn out or needs work. Fluids, filters, tires, gaskets, bushings: the habit is the same, and seeing the two together is the fastest way to learn the difference.
- **Calendar first, odometer second.** Fluids age whether the car moves or not. A car that sits a lot still needs the service, so the schedule is built around time.
- **Room for a helper.** Every guide names the jobs a kid can safely own and the ones that stay with the adult. Learning to read a level or compare a color is how a person starts to recognize normal, and a lesson repeated a few times sticks.
- **Honest verification.** Every guide ends with a Verification block that says which numbers were checked against which source, which came from the community, which came from hands-on work, and which are still marked with a warning flag (⚠️). Wrong specs multiply, so we show our work.

## Current scope

The 2021 Mazda MX-5 (ND2) Grand Touring, because that is the car the authors can verify by hand. The folder layout leaves room for sibling folders for other trims and generations later. A vague guide is a wrong guide, so we widen the scope only when we can verify the next car.

## How to read a warning flag

A line marked **⚠️** means we have not verified that value to our own standard. Treat it as a pointer to go check the factory manual, not as a fact. When a flag gets resolved, the Verification block records the source and the date.

## Safety

You are working on a machine that stops, steers, and holds itself up on jacks. Mistakes with brakes and lifting hurt people. Every guide has a safety block near the top. Read it, and use jack stands or a proper lift every time. These guides are shared in good faith as information, with no warranty, and you are responsible for your own work.

## Corrections

If you find a wrong number or a better explanation, open an issue or a pull request and include the source. A correction with a source beats a correction without one.
